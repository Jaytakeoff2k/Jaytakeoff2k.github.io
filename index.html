Projects

C++ Console Application

Anime Incremental

Anime Incremental is a C++ console game where the player creates a character, chooses a race, rolls for an anime-inspired skillset, travels between different worlds, fights enemies and bosses, earns coins, upgrades stats, and collects rare boss drops.

Key Features

  • Random skillset reroll system with six different anime-inspired skillsets.
  • Turn-based combat with damage, stamina costs, skipped turns, race abilities, and boss fights.
  • World travel, shop upgrades, inventory, coins, and rare boss drops with cosmetic rewards.

What I Learned

This project helped me improve my understanding of classes, vectors, functions, debugging, menus, and organizing a larger program across multiple files. One of the biggest challenges was getting different systems like combat, stamina, race abilities, inventory, and rewards to work together without breaking other parts of the game. Building and testing each system step by step helped me get better at debugging and problem-solving.
