Hello, I’m Jaden Johnson.

Hello, my name is Jaden. I'm new to the whole programming thing, just four months in, but I'm getting better at things like debugging, menu creation, and more. As a kid, I always wanted to make games because I loved playing them, but over time that dream died out. A few years ago, I was playing a game and was in its Discord, hearing about how the developers worked as a team and got things done while also getting special in-game benefits for helping. Seeing that made me want to be a part of something like that one day. One thing led to another, and it eventually led me to wanting to make games full-time.
